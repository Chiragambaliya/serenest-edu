§ Our approach

Built for India

Why Serenest Education exists — and who leads it.

Why this is different.

Five commitments Most online CE is content delivered into a void. Serenest Education is built so what you learn translates to what you do on Monday morning.
— 01

Indian cases. Indian context.

Every clinical case discussed is drawn from real Indian outpatient scenarios—joint family boundaries, cultural trauma, somatic distress, regional language adaptations—taught by local experts, not translated from foreign textbooks.

— 02

Real supervision, not just videos.

Cohort programs include active, small-group clinical supervision with senior practitioners. Bring your own anonymized cases, map formulations, address blindspots, and refine clinical boundaries.

— 03

Built by clinicians still in practice.

Our faculty actively treat patients every week. There are no dry theoretical lectures from professionals who left active clinical spaces decades ago. All case files are current.

— 04

Open access, serious standards.

All programmes are free during our launch phase. Cohort sizes stay small so supervision stays real — register via WhatsApp and we will confirm your seat with Zoom links and materials.

— 05

Certificates that carry weight.

Serenest Education operates under Serenest Pvt Ltd — a registered Private Limited company and an active clinical startup. Your completion certificate is issued under that company name, with a live platform behind it, giving you credentials employers, colleges, and supervisors can trust.

A 28-year-old Corporate Associate

"The client sits at the edge of the chair, continuously tapping their foot on the marble floor. They wear their clinical appointment slip in a tight grip, their knuckles turning pale white. When asked about their sleep patterns, they answer in rapid, short sentences: 'Sleep is... gone. Completely went. Not there. Four days maybe five? I watch the fan rotate. One, two, three. If I close my eyes, my brain says I will miss the deadlines. My manager is sending emails in my head even when the computer is shut. The emails are... everywhere. Ticking. Ticking like bombs.' Their facial muscles are visibly tense, and they occasionally glance towards the cabin door, starting at any distant corridor sound."
