Privacy

The Serenest Academy pages under /academy/ are static. They do not create an account, open a waitlist, take a payment, or store an enquiry on this site.

Contact leaves this website

WhatsApp links open a chat with +91 77776 36369. Email links open your mail programme to hello@serenest.academy. What you then send is handled by those services, under their own terms. Serenest Pvt Ltd receives the message only if you choose to send it.
