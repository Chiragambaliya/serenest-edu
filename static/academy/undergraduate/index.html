MBBS learning journey

These are simplified website groupings, not a statement of official NMC professional-year allocation. Check the applicable regulations before treating any pathway as NMC-aligned.

First Professional

Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry.

Emphasis: understanding normal human structure, function and biochemical mechanisms.

Proposed experiences: concept explanations, interactive anatomy, revision questions and introductory 3D models.

Explore 3D Anatomy

Second Professional

Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology.

Emphasis: connecting disease mechanisms, diagnostic principles and treatment foundations.

Proposed experiences: integrated disease explanations, case-based pharmacology and pathology models.

Pathology model preview

Third Professional

Community Medicine, Forensic Medicine, ENT, Ophthalmology.

Emphasis: population health, legal medicine and progressive clinical learning.

Proposed experiences: applied case discussions, examination scenarios and clinically relevant revision.

Final Professional

General Medicine, General Surgery, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Pediatrics, and allied clinical specialties.

Emphasis: clinical assessment, differential diagnosis, investigations and management reasoning.

Proposed experiences: simulated outpatient cases, structured viva practice and clinical reasoning exercises.

Understand, apply, practise, reflect

  1. Understand

    A concept is explained inside the subject it belongs to.

  2. Apply

    The same idea appears in a short simulated case.

  3. Practise

    A formative question or viva prompt asks the learner to use it.

  4. Reflect

    Educational feedback shows what was sound and what to revisit. Where a model exists, the feedback can point to it.

This is the planned method. None of these steps is open yet. The same idea is explained on Our Approach.
