A connected future for medical learning is taking shape.

From undergraduate foundations to specialist practice, we are building new ways to understand medicine, develop clinical reasoning and explore medical science through intelligent and physical learning experiences. No release date is announced.

The first planned release is for undergraduate learners, and only this much of it:

  1. Initial MBBS learning content
  2. AI-supported concept explanations
  3. A small introductory collection of faculty-reviewed simulated cases
  4. Basic MCQ and viva practice
  5. A 3D Medical Lab preview of proposed educational models

Postgraduate and doctor pathways stay on the site as future developments. The full catalogue is not launching at once.
